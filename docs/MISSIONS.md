# Adding missions, scenes and infographics

Several people (and agents) build content at the same time, so every new piece of content lives in its own files. The build (`src/build.py`) picks them up automatically, in alphabetical order:

| Folder | What goes there | Loaded |
|---|---|---|
| `src/missions/<id>.js` | One mission: `MISSIONS.<id>={...}` with its talks, docs, criteria, model, consent, memo, reveal, debrief and evening, plus its own glossary, cast, voices, Field Guide entries, lessons and passer-by chatter | after `missions.js` |
| `src/viz/<id>.js` | The mission's animated infographics (`VIZ.<kind>=(args)=>svg`) and its Future Table vignettes | after `infog.js` |
| `src/scenes/<key>.js` | A painted background: `PAINT.<key>={paint(ctx,w,h), overlay(ctx,w,h,t), focus, anchors, track}` | after `paint_boston.js` |
| `src/css/<id>.css` | Styles and keyframes for the files above; prefix every class with the mission or scene id | after `style.css` |
| `src/audio/<id>.js` | Generated by `tools/make_audio.py`; never edit by hand | after `audio.js` |

Never edit another mission's files. Edits to shared engine files (`game.js`, `missions.js`, `characters.js`, `style.css`, `props.js`) should be small, additive and listed in your hand-off notes so they merge cleanly.

## The mission object

Read the comment at the top of `src/missions.js` and the whole Maya mission (`MISSIONS.maya` in `src/missions.js`, its content in `src/content_boston.js`, its pictures in `src/infog.js` and `src/art.js`). A new mission has the same fields, but keeps its content inside the mission object instead of in shared tables:

```js
MISSIONS.rosa={id:'rosa',title:'…',career:'doctor',day:2,blurb:'…',after:['maya'],place:'…',
  cast:{rosa:{name:'Rosa Delgado',role:'Patient, age 68',skin:SKIN[3],hair:'#d8d4dc',hairStyle:'elder',shirt:'#e86a8a'}},
  voices:{rosa:'af_nicole'},
  glossary:{'blood pressure':'How hard your blood pushes on the walls of your blood vessels.'},  // keys lowercase
  guide:{'who-pays':{title:'…',track:'Incentives',text:'…',real:'True today: …'}},
  lessons:{bp:{title:'How blood pressure works',steps:[{t:'…',viz:'…',cap:'…'}],quiz:[{q:'…',opts:['…','…','…'],a:0,why:'…'}]}},
  chatter:{kitchen:['…']},
  talks:{…}, docs:[…], react:{…}, criteria:[…], controls:[…], start:{…},
  planName(c){…}, outcomes(c,knows){…}, truth(){…}, knows(){…}, why(c){…}, whyGuide(c){…},
  vignette(kind){…},            // Future Table and reveal pictures for this mission's outcome kinds
  consent:{scene:'kitchen',place:'…',…}, memo:{…}, reveal:{…}, trap(c){…}, byteGood:'…', evening(){…}, brief(){…}, canned(q){…},
  custom:{stepName(){…}},       // optional new mechanics, used by flow steps {name:'stepName',k:'custom'}
  lines:[{who:'byte',text:'…'}],// any other line spoken with playLine (so it gets recorded)
  eveningScene:'night', eveningPlace:'Home · Boston · 9:10 PM'};
```

- `flow` steps: `{name, k:'talk'|'custom'|'bench'|'criteria'|'table'|'consent'|'memo'|'reveal'|'debrief'|'evening', talk, scene, place, toast, music}`. Step names must be unique within the mission.
- Top-level code in mission files must be plain data and function definitions. `tools/make_audio.py` evaluates `art.js`, `content_boston.js`, `missions.js` and `src/missions/*.js` in node, with no DOM.
- Code that needs things defined later (for example `PROPS` from `props.js`) goes in a `LATE.push(()=>{…})` hook, which runs once at startup.
- `S` is the save state. Mission-specific state goes under `S.notes` or `S.m_<id>` so it never collides.
- `outcomes(c,knows)` returns `[{kind, p, cap, flags:{criterionId:0|1}}]` whose `p` values sum to 1. The Future Table builds 10 typical futures from these numbers, so keep them realistic and consistent with every number written in the documents.

## Writing rules (from the family)

- Reading level: about 7th grade. Short sentences. Every hard word or phrase is `[[marked]]` and has a glossary entry, including phrases like `[[heals straight]]`.
- Every document sentence is its own step with its own looping animated picture (`viz`). The pictures carry the meaning of the sentence, so a child could follow the story from the pictures alone. Never reuse one picture for a whole document.
- 2040 is a realistic world after superintelligence (the "AI 2040" Plan A world): AI is excellent and runs big organizations, and that is mundane. People talk normally; nobody marvels at the AI. Humans add goals, consent, local knowledge, judgment and accountability.
- Byte is genuinely brilliant and helpful. It raises the key consideration and asks what matters, then recommends. It does not dumb itself down, and it does not make the decision for the player.
- Real places by default, with real-sounding details. Every disaster, villain lair and villain plot is in a made-up place (Calder Bay, Marrow Point). No real living people. No brand names.
- The player is accountable: the mission ends with a written decision that she signs.
- No clutter: no explanatory subtitles, eyebrow text or long instructions on screen.
- Never ask the player for real personal details.
- Kid-safe: villains fail comically; dangerous details stay vague.

## Characters

`portrait(o)` draws an anime bust from options: `name, role, skin (SKIN[0..5]), hair (color), hairStyle ('short'|'buzz'|'bun'|'braids'|'long'|'elder'), shirt, coat, glasses, accessory ('earring'), age ('kid'), masc, eye`, plus `mood` (`smile happy neutral worried sad surprised stern think`). More styles are being added: `ponytail`, `afro`, `bob`, `curls`, `bald` (with `beard: 'full'|'short'|'mustache'`), `headscarf`, `age:'elder'` (wrinkles); until they land, unknown styles draw as `short`. `posedPerson(kind, castOptions)` (coming; check with `typeof posedPerson==='function'`) draws a full-body figure with its feet at (0,0), about 70 units tall, in poses `stand wave walk sit dance cane thumbsup sad think cheer`. `mini(x,y,o)` and `posedGirl(kind,o)` already exist.

## Voices

Do not run the TTS yourself. Pick a voice for each cast member in `voices:{}` and the lines are recorded centrally with `tools/make_audio.py`. Kokoro voice names: `af_alloy af_aoede af_bella af_heart af_jessica af_kore af_nicole af_nova af_river af_sarah af_sky am_adam am_echo am_eric am_fenrir am_liam am_michael am_onyx am_puck bf_alice bf_emma bf_isabella bf_lily bm_daniel bm_fable bm_george bm_lewis`. Children use role names `kid_girl` and `kid_boy` (pitched and formant-shifted, defined in `tools/make_audio.py`). Elders can use `elder_woman` and `elder_man`.

## Painted scenes

Read the header of `src/paint_boston.js`: seeded painting utilities, a static `paint` that the game caches, and a calm `overlay` animation. A new scene file wraps its code in an IIFE, can copy the utilities it needs, and must paint identically every time. Leave the lower third calm enough for subtitles and the middle clear for a character portrait. Set `track` to one of `title morning hospital work table win night`. Tappable props for a scene go in a `LATE.push(()=>{PROPS.<key>=[…]})` hook in the scene file (see `src/props.js` for the prop format).

## Check your work

```sh
python3 src/build.py dist/pathmakers.html
node tests/flow_test.js dist/pathmakers.html                    # every mission plays end to end, no errors
node tools/mission_shots.js dist/pathmakers.html /tmp/shots <id> # phone-size screenshots of every step of one mission
```

Look at every screenshot (contact sheets help), fix what looks wrong, and repeat until nothing is left to improve.
